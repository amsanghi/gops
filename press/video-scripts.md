# Video / reel / short scripts

> All scripts are formatted for a phone screen recording with on-screen captions. You don't need a camera, voice-over, or editing software. Use any built-in screen recorder. Burn captions in with [CapCut](https://capcut.com), [Descript](https://descript.com), or [Veed.io](https://veed.io) — all have free tiers.

**Aspect:** record 1080×1920 (vertical) for Reels/Shorts/TikTok. Crop to 1080×1080 for square Instagram feed.

## Tools / how to actually record these

**Built-in (no extra app needed):**
- **iPhone:** Control Center → Screen Recording. Captions: in iMovie or [CapCut Mobile](https://www.capcut.com/) (free).
- **Android:** Built-in screen recorder (varies). Captions: CapCut, [InShot](https://inshot.com).
- **Desktop:** Mac → Cmd-Shift-5. Windows → Game Bar (Win-G). Captions via [Veed.io](https://veed.io) or [Descript](https://descript.com) (free tier).

**Free trending music sources:**
- [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music) (no attribution required, commercial use)
- [YouTube Audio Library](https://www.youtube.com/audiolibrary)
- [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org)

**Captioning shortcuts:**
- Use **CapCut "Auto Captions"** — type the script, press one button, captions appear word-by-word
- Or **Descript** — paste the script, get a video with subtitles

**Avoid (will get you copyright struck):**
- Songs from the regular charts (Spotify trends, Billboard etc.)
- Disney / Pixar / movie audio
- Any audio you didn't get the source license to

## Quality bar

A 60-second phone-screen recording with hand-typed captions and free Pixabay background music **outperforms** a $1000 production for indie game launches. Save your production budget for after you have an audience.

Ship the minimum-viable reel TODAY. You can re-record once you know what works.
