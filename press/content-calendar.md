# 4-week launch plan

Times are suggestions. The big rule: **ship something every day** for the first two weeks. Less polished is better than late.

> 📌 Don't worry about being on every platform. Start with **2 or 3 you actually use**, then expand. Most launches die from spreading too thin.

## Week 0 — prep (1-2 hours)

- [ ] Create accounts (only ones you don't have): X / Bsky / Threads / TikTok / Instagram / Mastodon
- [ ] Set bios to one of the lines from [`pitch.md`](./pitch.md)
- [ ] Pin the launch tweet (planned for Day 1)
- [ ] Add a Linktree-style page or use one tweet as your "link in bio"
- [ ] Record **one** 30-second reel using Script 1 from [`video-scripts.md`](./video-scripts.md)
- [ ] Capture the screenshots in [`screenshots-guide.md`](./screenshots-guide.md)

## Week 1 — launch

### Day 1 (Monday) — wide announcement

- 9 AM ET: **Tweet/Bsky/Threads** the main launch post (Variant A from social-copy)
- 9:05 AM: post the 30s reel to **Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts** with the launch caption
- 11 AM ET: post to **r/WebGames** — text post, the long-form variant
- Evening: reply to all comments. Pin questions you can't answer immediately.

> Why Monday morning? Web devs and game/strategy folks are checking feeds before they hop into work. Best discovery window on Twitter/Reddit.

### Day 2 (Tuesday) — broader Reddit + nerd hook

- Post **r/boardgames** with the more boardgame-flavored variant
- Post **r/cardgames**
- Cross-post to **r/incremental_games** (use the streak/progression angle)
- Twitter thread with the "Did you know Goofspiel was a 1935 game theory artifact" angle
- Bsky: same as Twitter

### Day 3 (Wednesday) — Hacker News

- 11 AM ET: **Show HN** post (from social-copy.md). Aim for "Show HN: GOPS – A No-Luck Card Game in Your Browser (P2P Multiplayer)"
- Critical: be available for the next 4-6 hours to reply to every comment. HN responses live or die in the first 90 minutes.

### Day 4 (Thursday) — visual content + tutorial

- Post **Script 3 (history)** as a reel/short
- Post a tutorial **Script 6** as a longer YouTube Short
- Threads: "Did you know the Prisoner's Dilemma guy invented a card game?"

### Day 5 (Friday) — community engagement

- Comment on every existing GOPS / Goofspiel video on YouTube — see [`engagement-strategy.md`](./engagement-strategy.md)
- Reply to existing Reddit threads about Goofspiel (search by keyword)
- Cross-promote in indie game communities (Indie Hackers, IndieGames Reddit, gamedev Discord servers)

### Weekend

- Quieter. Post the **bullet-mode reaction** Script 5 on TikTok (Saturday is good for TikTok)
- Saturday afternoon: post a screenshot of an interesting daily challenge result
- Sunday: re-share Day 1 launch tweet from a different angle ("forgot to mention coach mode flags your mistakes")

## Week 2 — sustain + Product Hunt

### Day 8 (Monday) — Product Hunt launch

- 12:01 AM PT: launch on **Product Hunt**
- Submit through [Product Hunt's interface](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/new)
- Use copy from social-copy.md
- Tag friendly creators (use Product Hunt's tagging) so they see it

### Day 9-12 — featured-mode showcase week

- Day 9: post a 15s clip of Bullet mode → trigger speed kink
- Day 10: post a clip of party room with voice chat
- Day 11: post a clip showing coach mode insights
- Day 12: post a daily challenge result, screenshot of share grid

### Day 13-14 — UGC bait

- Post a screenshot of your bid heatmap with caption "Show me yours"
- Encourage replies showing their stats / wins

## Week 3 — community + content

### Day 15-21 — slow-roll posting

- Once-daily post, no specific schedule
- **Reply to comments** on previous posts (this is now your biggest engagement source)
- Find 1-2 streamers or YouTubers in the card-game / browser-game space; cold email them using template in social-copy.md
- Submit to TLDR Newsletter, Webdesign Weekly, JavaScript Weekly (it's vanilla JS + zero build — they like that)
- Submit to [LowercaseS.com](https://www.lowercases.com/) (free static site sharing)

## Week 4+ — evergreen content cadence

Pick a sustainable rhythm. Examples:

- **"Daily challenge result of the day"** — automated brag post (you play the daily, screenshot the share grid, post with one line of commentary)
- **"Coach insight of the week"** — screenshot an interesting Coach finding
- **Tip Tuesday** — share one strategic tip ("Don't bid your K on a 1-prize")
- **Feature Friday** — a 15s clip of one mode or feature
- **Subreddit + Discord lurking** — be useful. Don't spam. People discover you organically.

## Anti-patterns (don't do these)

- ❌ Same tweet/post identical across platforms. Adapt the tone.
- ❌ Posting and immediately leaving. Stay around for the first hour to reply.
- ❌ Cold-DMing influencers asking them to share. Email or public reply.
- ❌ Promoting in subreddits without a strong "I made this" angle. Mods will ban-spam.
- ❌ Buying followers / engagement pods. Will hurt long-term reach.
- ❌ Worrying about looking polished. The dev showing their actual face / hands / screen always converts better than overproduced marketing.

---

## Definition of "success"

This isn't going to go viral overnight. Reasonable targets after 4 weeks:

| Metric | Reasonable goal | Stretch goal |
|---|---|---|
| GitHub stars | 50 | 500 |
| Daily unique players | 50 | 1,000 |
| Twitter followers | 30 | 300 |
| Reddit posts that hit 100+ upvotes | 1 | 3 |
| YouTube subscribers (if posting reels) | 20 | 200 |
| Issues / PRs from contributors | 1 | 10 |

The play is to build a small but engaged group of regular players + contributors. Compound interest over the next 6-12 months matters more than week-1 numbers.
