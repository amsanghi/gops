## Longer description (~200 words, for press / Product Hunt / a "read more" link)

GOPS, also known as Goofspiel or The Game of Pure Strategy, is one of the most elegant card games ever invented — a 1930s creation from a Princeton mathematician that game theorists still cite as the canonical example of a simultaneous-move game. The premise is brutal in its simplicity: both players hold an identical hand of A through K, a prize deck is shuffled face-down, and each round both players secretly bid one card to claim the revealed prize. There's no luck. No drawing. Nothing hidden but intent.

This version brings the game online, free, with no account required. Play solo against a dozen AI personalities, take on the daily challenge, climb the tournament ladder, or invite up to seven friends into a party room with text, voice, and video chat. There's coach-mode post-game analysis, replay scrubbing, custom themes, and a full PWA install. The whole thing is open source, runs in your browser, and uses peer-to-peer WebRTC so there's no backend — just you and your opponent.

## Differentiators (what makes THIS version stand out)

- **Free, no signup, no app store** — just a URL
- **Voice and video chat** built in (over PeerJS/WebRTC, peer-to-peer)
- **Party rooms** for 2–8 players, not just 1v1
- **Coach mode** — post-game analysis flags your suboptimal turns with reasoning
- **Replay tools** — scrub past games, export as `.webm` video
- **No-scroll UI** that fits any device, optimized for Bullet (3s/bid) mode
- **PWA** — installable, works offline
- **Open source** (MIT) — contributors welcome
- **Privacy-first** — all state in browser localStorage, no tracking
