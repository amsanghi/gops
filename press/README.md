## What I can do vs. what only **you** can do

### I (the README) can give you
- All written content (posts, captions, scripts, descriptions)
- The launch strategy and timing
- A press kit page (in the repo, just commit it)
- An OG / Twitter preview image (HTML you can screenshot)
- A list of communities + specific subreddits to engage
- Email templates to pitch to writers/YouTubers

### You will have to do these yourself
| Task | Why |
|---|---|
| **Create accounts** on the platforms you don't have | Security: only you should hold credentials |
| **Record videos / reels** | Needs your voice, camera, hands |
| **Post the content** | Auth + your tone-of-voice + community responses |
| **Reply to comments** | Authentic engagement matters more than schedule |
| **Apply to Product Hunt / TLDR** | Forms ask for personal info |

You don't need any of this to be polished. **A single Reddit post + a 30-second phone screen-recording with handwritten subtitles can outperform a glossy ad.**

## Quick start — your next 60 minutes

1. **Read [`pitch.md`](./pitch.md)** — pick your favorite one-liner.
2. **Open [`og-image.html`](./og-image.html)** in a browser, take a screenshot at 1200×630, save as `press/og-image.png`. Add to repo.
3. **Read [`launch-checklist.md`](./launch-checklist.md)** — do the first day's items.
4. **Post on Reddit** (`r/WebGames`, `r/boardgames`, `r/incremental_games`) using [`social-copy.md`](./social-copy.md). Reddit doesn't punish text posts.
5. **Tweet / Bsky / Threads** the launch post.
6. **Record one 30-second screen capture** of a Vs AI game ending with a comeback win. Caption: "No luck. No randomness. Just nerve." Post everywhere as a reel/short.

That's an entire launch sequence with zero video production.
