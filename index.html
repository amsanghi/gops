About GOPS

Game of Pure Strategy — Goofspiel to its friends — is a card game with no luck in it, studied at Princeton in the 1930s alongside the birth of game theory. You and your opponent hold the same hand, A–K. A prize turns face up; you each bid one card in secret; both bids reveal at once and the higher one takes the prize. The card you spent never comes back. After thirteen rounds, the bigger pile of points wins.
